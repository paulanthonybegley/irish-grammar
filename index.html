🍀 Irish Grammar

Six interactive lessons on the tenses of Irish — continuous and simple — with conjugation tables, drills and ABAIR pronunciation.

Fáilte! · About this course

An interactive companion to the YouTube playlist “Irish Grammar” by Stories in Irish. The playlist covers the three continuous tenses (present, past, future) and the three simple tenses (habitual present, future, past). Each lesson turns the video into an active practice programme: rules and conjugation tables, flashcards, fill-in-the-blanks and a quiz — with Irish audio from ABAIR.

Click any green Irish word to hear it spoken. Your progress is saved automatically.
